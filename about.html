Our Story

Passion, precision,
and a love
for great food.

We started as a small family kitchen with a big dream — to bring restaurant-quality dining to every celebration, boardroom, and backyard in South Africa.

Who We Are

More than caterers — we're storytellers through food.

Full Table Service was founded on a simple belief: that every meal is an opportunity to create a memory. Over more than a decade, we've grown from a home kitchen to a full-service catering company with a team of passionate chefs, event coordinators, and hospitality professionals.


We work across Johannesburg and all provinces, bringing meticulous attention to detail, seasonal menus, and genuine warmth to every event we touch.

Our Journey

How we got here.

2012

Founded in Johannesburg

Started as a boutique dinner-party service by our founder and head chef, operating out of a home kitchen in Gauteng.

2015

First Corporate Contract

Secured a recurring corporate catering contract, marking our expansion beyond private events into the business sector.

2018

Full-Service Team Assembled

Grew to a team of 20+ including dedicated chefs, front-of-house staff, and an in-house event coordinator.

2021

National Coverage

Expanded service capabilities to cover all nine provinces, partnering with regional suppliers and local talent across the country.

2024

500+ Events Milestone

Celebrated our 500th event — a milestone that reflects hundreds of families, couples, and companies who trusted us with their most important days.
