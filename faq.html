Booking

How far in advance should I book?
+

For standard events (private parties, corporate lunches) we recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance. For weddings and large-scale functions, 3–6 months ahead is ideal. Popular dates — especially December, March, and year-end periods — fill up quickly, so the sooner you reach out, the better.

How do I make a booking?
+

Simply fill in our inquiry form on the Contact page with your event details — date, guest count, type of event, and any special requirements. We'll respond within 24 hours with a personalised quote and next steps. No obligation until you sign the contract and pay the deposit.

Can I book for the same week or next day?
+

In some cases, yes — depending on availability and the scale of your event. We do accommodate last-minute bookings for smaller events when our schedule allows. Contact us as soon as possible and we'll let you know our availability. An emergency booking fee may apply for events booked within 5 days.

Do you service outside of Johannesburg?
+

Yes! While we're based in Johannesburg, we regularly service events across Gauteng and throughout South Africa. Travel and logistics fees apply for events more than 50km from our base in Johannesburg. For out-of-province events, contact us for a customised quote that includes travel and accommodation for our team.

Payment

What is the deposit structure?
+

We require a 30–50% deposit upon signing the service contract to secure your booking and date. The remaining balance is invoiced and due within 7 days after your event has been completed and signed off. For larger events, the split may be negotiated — this will be detailed in your contract.

What payment methods do you accept?
+

We accept EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer), credit and debit card payments, and select mobile payment platforms. Our preferred method is EFT as it avoids processing fees. All payment details are included on your invoice. We do not accept cash for bookings.

What is your cancellation policy?
+

Cancellations made more than 30 days before the event will receive a full refund of the deposit minus a 10% administration fee. Cancellations between 15–30 days will receive a 50% refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days will forfeit the deposit entirely. All cancellations must be submitted in writing via email. Please refer to your signed service contract for the full policy.

Are there any hidden fees?
+

No. Our quotes are fully itemised and transparent. Any potential additional costs — such as travel fees, last-minute menu changes, or emergency staffing — are communicated to you upfront before your contract is signed. You will never receive a surprise charge on your final invoice.

Menus & Food

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?
+

Absolutely — dietary accommodation is a core part of our service. We cater for vegan, vegetarian, halal, kosher, gluten-free, nut-free, dairy-free, and other allergen-specific requirements. The number of dietary options included varies by package, but we always ensure every guest is catered for. Please provide a full breakdown of dietary needs when making your inquiry.

Can I customise my menu?
+

Yes — all menus are customisable to varying degrees depending on your package. Our Signature and Prestige packages include consultation sessions where we work with you to build a menu from scratch. Essential package clients can swap dishes within the existing menu. We also offer a full custom menu design service for Prestige bookings, including a private tasting session.

Do you offer tasting sessions before the event?
+

Yes! Our Prestige package includes a complimentary tasting session for up to 4 guests at our kitchen or a venue of your choice. For Signature package clients, tastings can be arranged for an additional fee of R850, which is credited against your final invoice. Essential package clients are welcome to view and taste from our existing sample menu at one of our tasting events (dates published quarterly).

Do you use fresh, local ingredients?
+

Yes — we're committed to seasonal, locally sourced ingredients as much as possible. We work with a network of trusted South African producers and suppliers. Menus are refreshed seasonally to reflect what's fresh and available. Imported specialty ingredients are used where local alternatives don't meet our quality standard.

Logistics

Do you supply equipment and staff?
+

Yes — all packages include professional service staff, crockery, cutlery, and serving equipment. Premium packages include upgraded tableware, linen, and a dedicated event coordinator. We bring everything needed to serve your guests professionally, and collect all equipment at the end of the event. You don't need to source anything separately.

When does your team arrive and leave?
+

Our team typically arrives 2–3 hours before the event start time to set up, prepare, and be ready well before your guests arrive. We stay through the entire event including service and clearing, and typically depart 1–2 hours after the event concludes once all equipment has been packed and the space is clean.

Do you clean up after the event?
+

Yes — full cleanup is included in all packages. This covers clearing all tables, washing all serving equipment we've brought, packing everything out, removing any food waste, and leaving the kitchen and dining areas clean and tidy. Prestige package includes an additional post-event deep clean option.

Can you work in a venue without a kitchen?
+

Yes — we have mobile kitchen equipment and can operate in marquees, outdoor spaces, warehouses, and venues without built-in catering facilities. We'll assess your venue during the planning phase and advise on any additional equipment or logistics required. There may be an additional equipment hire fee for off-grid venues.

Events

What is the maximum number of guests you can serve?
+

We regularly cater for events of up to 500 guests. For events exceeding 500 guests, please contact us directly so we can assess staffing, logistics, and equipment requirements. We have partnered with trusted colleagues in the industry to support larger-scale events when necessary.

Can you handle both food and beverage service?
+

Yes — we offer full bar and beverage service as an add-on to all packages. This includes a licensed bartender, glassware, a curated cocktail and mocktail menu, and soft drink alternatives. We can also provide wine and champagne service as part of dinner service. Note that we require a licenced venue or temporary liquor licence for events where alcohol is served.

What happens if my guest count changes?
+

Final guest numbers must be confirmed no later than 7 days before the event. We can accommodate increases of up to 15% above your contracted number with reasonable notice, though additional charges will apply. Decreases of up to 10% can be accommodated without penalty if notified 7+ days before the event.

Still have questions?

Our team is available Monday to Saturday, 8am–6pm. We typically respond to all inquiries within a few hours during business hours. Don't hesitate to reach out — there's no such thing as a silly question when it comes to your event.
